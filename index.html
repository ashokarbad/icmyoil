    Why pressed fresh beats bottled

    Store oil can sit for months and is often refined or blended. Ours goes from press to bottle while you're standing there.

    • You see exactly what goes in

      Your own seeds, or ours that you can inspect first. Nothing is mixed in after pressing.

    • Unrefined, with nothing added

      No chemical solvents, bleaching or preservatives. The oil keeps the natural colour, taste and aroma of the seed.

    • Fair, transparent pricing

      You pay a per-kg processing charge. Bring your own seeds and you control the quality and the cost.

    • Glass, not plastic

      Fridge-grade glass bottles at the lowest price we can manage — or bring your own container and save ₹30.

    • Oils your grandparents used

      Kalonji, safflower, pumpkin seed and others that are hard to find fresh. If we don't stock a seed, we can usually source it from the market yard.

    What regulars say

    Questions people ask at the counter

    Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

    Can I bring my own seeds?

    Yes — that's how most customers use us. Bring groundnut, coconut, mustard, sesame or other seeds and we press them while you wait. You pay only the processing charge per kg.

    What if I don't have seeds?

    We stock seeds at wholesale rates for pressing. The final price depends on that day's seed price, so message us for the current rate.

    How much oil will I get from 1 kg?

    It depends on the seed and its quality. As a guide: groundnut gives 30–45%, 1 kg of dry coconut gives about 500–600 ml, and 1 kg of kalonji gives about 200–250 ml. Use the calculator at the top for a quick estimate.

    How long does it take?

    About 10–15 minutes per litre of oil.

    What's the difference between hot press and cold press?

    Cold pressing keeps the seed at a lower temperature, which preserves more of the natural flavour. Hot pressing usually gives a little more oil. You choose — we'll explain the trade-off for your seed at the counter.

    Do I need to bring a container?

    It helps — bring your own clean container and save ₹30. Otherwise we fill fridge-grade glass bottles, or pouches for travel, which are ₹25 cheaper than glass.

    Is fresh-pressed oil good for frying?

    Groundnut and mustard oil are traditional choices for Indian frying and tempering. Like any unrefined oil, avoid overheating it until it smokes, and don't reuse frying oil many times.

    Why does it smell stronger than store oil?

    Refining strips out smell and colour. Unrefined oil keeps the natural aroma of the seed, so mustard smells like mustard and groundnut smells nutty.

    How should I store it?

    In a cool, dark place, in glass or steel. Fresh oil has no preservatives, so press the quantity you'll use in the next few weeks rather than stocking up for months.

    Why does it cost more than refined oil?

    Small-batch pressing is slower and yields less than industrial extraction, and nothing is blended in to stretch it. What you pay for is whole seed, pressed fresh.
